About · Atlanta, GA

Denim with a past.

Bone Denim started with a pile of old jeans and a sewing machine. Each pair is sourced, taken apart and reworked by hand, so no two come out the same.

We measure every pair and list it on its own. Pick yours, choose how it's finished, and we make it in 10–14 days.

How do I know if it fits?+

Lay a pair you love flat and measure waist, rise, inseam, thigh and leg opening. Compare with the measurements on each pair. Every pair is listed with its own numbers.

How does customization work?+

Choose your hem, distressing, patch and initials before adding the pair to your bag. We finish it by hand after you order, which takes 10–14 days.

Shipping+

Ships from Atlanta, GA. US orders arrive in 3–5 business days after the pair is finished. Example policy, to confirm with Bone Denim.

Returns+

Pairs without customization can be returned within 14 days. Customized pairs are made for you and are final sale. Example policy, to confirm with Bone Denim.

Will a sold pair come back?+

No. Every pair is one of one. Join the waitlist to hear about the next drop first.
